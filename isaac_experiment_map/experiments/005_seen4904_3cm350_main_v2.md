## 1. Context & Motivation

### Why the Dataset Became 4,904 Episodes
Under the updated Isaac reaching protocol (3.0 cm threshold, 350 control ticks, no dwell), the dataset was derived directly from the union of Seen4000 (Round 0) and completed HARD1000 (Round 2) raw collections (total source pool of 5,000 episodes).
- **Inclusion Criterion**: An episode is included if and only if its binary outcome under the new protocol is mathematically proven with 100% certainty from raw stored scalar minimums and episode lengths.
- **Exclusions (96 episodes)**: 45 Seen episodes and 51 Hard episodes entered the region $(0.020\text{ m}, 0.030\text{ m}]$ after tick 350, but because intermediate continuous trajectories were not logged prior to tick 350, their exact first crossing time relative to tick 350 cannot be proven. They were strictly excluded to maintain 100% ground-truth label integrity.
- **Final Exact Pool**: **4,904 episodes** (4,387 success, 517 failure, 96,813 rows).

---

## 2. Protocol & Architecture

### Protocol
- **Distance threshold**: 0.030 m (3.0 cm)
- **Control rate**: 30 Hz (Physics rate: 120 Hz, Decimation: 4)
- **Maximum horizon**: 350 control ticks (11.67 s)
- **Execution**: H10 (10 actions per decision query, maximum 35 queries / `decision_index <= 34`)
- **Dwell**: NO DWELL (first $\le 0.030$ m crossing terminates immediately as success).

### Model Architecture & Optimization
- **Architecture**: `SeqRiskModel` (hist_dim=21, action_dim=7, static_dim=51, width=128, layers=3, heads=4, ffn=512, dropout=0.1; 27 transformer tokens = 1 CLS + 16 history + 10 action).
- **Optimization**: 10 epochs, AdamW (lr 2e-4, wd 1e-4), batch size 512, grad clip 1.0, weighted BCE loss (`pos_weight = 4.3453`).
- **Model Checkpoint**: `isaac_seen4904_h10_topk8_temporal_3cm350_main_v2/model.pt` (SHA-256: `00ad096a9ca38577366e992e1d7f8aa25b6f56f2f2bd7354abce1790baf890f1`).
- **Train Normalization**: `norm.npz` (SHA-256: `6fbd2b221c4490c975e3e1492c96a9e879a586f0b3a4c4eaf97ea05920960341`).
