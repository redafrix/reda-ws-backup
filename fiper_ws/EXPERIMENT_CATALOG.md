# Experiment Catalog Entry Point

Open [`experiment_catalog/README.md`](experiment_catalog/README.md) for the
distributed Batman, Bob, Dean, and Sam experiment inventory.

The catalog is the source of truth for what a result actually means. Raw files
remain at their original paths for reproducibility.

## New H10 Ablation (2026-06-08)

The H10 specialization campaign on Bob successfully demonstrated that aggressive thresholds (0.3) on the TopK8 detector provide a significant success boost (+5% on Task 6) compared to conservative thresholds and the greedy baseline. The H10-retrained detector is superior to the older pre-H10 variants.
