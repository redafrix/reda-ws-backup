Because of the Tailnet shift and hostname confusion, my `bob` and `sam` bash aliases broke. My agent fixed this by updating my local `~/.bashrc` and `~/.ssh/config` to use explicit Tailscale IPs and the `-F` flag to bypass local DNS pollution:
